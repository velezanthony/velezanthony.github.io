## Cookies

I only use analytics cookies, and only if you accept them. Until you do, Google Tag Manager is not even loaded.

<div class="table-scroll" tabindex="0">

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| \_ga | Google Ireland Limited | Tells one visitor from another | 2 years | Analytics, third party |
| \_ga\_\<ID> | Google Ireland Limited | Keeps the state of the Google Analytics 4 session | 2 years | Analytics, third party |

The theme, the language and your choice live in the browser’s storage. They are not cookies and need no consent.

You can change your mind at any time with “Cookie preferences”, at the foot of the page. Rejecting after having accepted deletes the Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. You can also delete them from your browser’s settings.

## Your rights

Over your data you can exercise these rights:

- Access: knowing what data of yours is handled.
- Rectification: correcting it if it is wrong.
- Erasure: asking for it to be deleted.
- Objection: opposing a way of handling it.
- Restriction: asking for it to be frozen.
- Portability: receiving it in a format you can reuse.

To exercise them, write to me at [velezanthony2000@gmail.com](mailto:velezanthony2000@gmail.com). If what you want is held by GitHub, Google or another service, you can ask them too. You can withdraw your consent whenever you like; what was handled until then remains valid.

If you think I have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([AEPD](https://www.aepd.es)).
